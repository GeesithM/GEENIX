SEO Frequently Asked Questions

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO focuses on the technical aspects of your website that affect search engine crawling, indexing, and ranking. This includes site speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile responsiveness, structured data, XML sitemaps, canonical tags, and proper HTML structure.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term investment. Technical improvements can show results within weeks, but meaningful organic traffic growth typically takes 3 to 6 months. Results depend on competition, content quality, and the current state of your website.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. No legitimate SEO provider can guarantee specific rankings. Search results are determined by hundreds of factors. What we guarantee is technically excellent implementation, best-practice SEO, and measurable improvements to your website's visibility and performance.

What's the difference between technical SEO and content SEO?

Technical SEO ensures search engines can crawl, index, and understand your website efficiently. Content SEO focuses on creating useful, relevant content that matches search intent. Both are necessary for sustainable organic growth. GEENIX excels at technical SEO and can advise on content strategy.
