About GEENIX — Engineering-First Web Development

We are a Sri Lanka-based web development and digital solutions team. We build high-speed websites, custom web applications, and resilient digital architectures that help forward-thinking businesses thrive online.

Talk to Our Team Explore Our Work

Our Philosophy: Real Engineering, No Gimmicks

Too many agencies deliver bloated templates, slow page speeds, and black-box promises that leave clients locked in. At GEENIX, we believe in radical transparency, clean code, and measured outcomes.

Performance First

Every website we ship is engineered for sub-second response times and passes Google Core Web Vitals out of the box.

Clean, Maintainable Code

We write standards-compliant HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React without relying on brittle page builders or opaque plugins.

Total Client Ownership

You own 100% of your source code, domain, repository, and assets. No hostage scenarios or proprietary vendor lock-in.

Direct Communication

Work directly with the software engineers and designers building your project. No confusing intermediary account managers.

How We Work

A proven, five-stage delivery workflow designed to keep projects on time, on budget, and true to your vision:

1. Discovery & Strategy

We analyze your business objectives, target audience, technical constraints, and search opportunity to build a clear scope and timeline.

2. UI/UX Architecture

We create clean wireframes and interactive Figma prototypes, refining user flows and information hierarchy before writing any code.

3. Agile Engineering

We build with modern frameworks (React, Next.js, custom WordPress), keeping you updated through continuous staging previews.

4. Quality & Speed Audit

We test across real devices, validate accessibility (WCAG), audit security, and optimize Core Web Vitals before launch.

Based in Colombo, Working Worldwide

Our headquarters is in Colombo, Sri Lanka (GMT+5:30). We serve growing Sri Lankan businesses as well as international clients across Europe, Australia, the Middle East, and North America who appreciate engineering excellence, clear communication, and cost-effective value.
