Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a web development project cost in Sri Lanka?

Project costs depend on scope, features, and technology. Simple corporate websites start from an accessible budget, while full-stack React/Next.js web apps and e-commerce platforms are quoted transparently based on milestone deliverables. Contact us for a free, itemized estimate.

How long does it take to design and launch a website?

A standard business website typically takes 2 to 3 weeks. Custom web applications, e-commerce stores, and complex Next.js platforms usually take 4 to 8 weeks including design, QA testing, and search engine setup.

Can you help improve our existing website's speed and SEO?

Yes. We offer comprehensive website performance remediation and SEO audits to diagnose why your site is slow or failing to rank on Google. We fix Core Web Vitals, implement Schema markup, and optimize code structure.

Do you provide maintenance and hosting support after launch?

Absolutely. We provide ongoing maintenance packages covering security updates, content revisions, speed monitoring, automated backups, and technical support to ensure your site runs flawlessly 24/7.
