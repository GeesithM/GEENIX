WooCommerce Development FAQ

Can you connect local Sri Lankan banks and payment gateways?

Yes. We have hands-on experience integrating approved Sri Lankan payment gateways including PayHere, WebXpay, and direct IPG solutions from major commercial banks (Commercial Bank, Sampath Vishwa/IPG, HNB, Seylan). We also configure multi-currency support and cash-on-delivery (COD) workflows.

How do you ensure our WooCommerce store doesn't run slowly?

Slow WooCommerce stores are typically caused by bloated multi-purpose themes and overloaded plugins. At GEENIX, we write lightweight, custom-coded themes, utilize server-level Redis object caching, configure automated image compression, and optimize database queries to ensure rapid page load times even during flash sales.

Can my staff easily manage inventory, orders, and products?

Absolutely. We provide a customized, streamlined administrative dashboard and full onboarding training for your team. You can easily add new products, adjust stock levels, print shipping manifests, and track customer shipments without any coding knowledge.
