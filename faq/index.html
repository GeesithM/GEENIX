Pricing & Timelines

How much does a website cost in Sri Lanka?

Website costs vary depending on architecture and custom requirements. A professional corporate website starts between LKR 60,000 and LKR 150,000. Custom eCommerce platforms with local payment gateways (PayHere, WebXpay) and inventory management typically range between LKR 150,000 and LKR 400,000+. We provide fixed-scope proposals with no hidden fees.

How long does a website project take from start to launch?

Standard corporate and marketing websites typically take 2 to 4 weeks. Larger eCommerce stores or custom React web applications take 4 to 8 weeks. Clear milestone deadlines are established during the discovery phase so you always know what to expect.

What payment terms do you offer?

Our standard milestone structure is 40% initial deposit to begin design and architecture, 30% upon approval of the working staging build, and the remaining 30% upon final quality sign-off and deployment to your live domain.

Technology & Process

Do you use page builders like Elementor or Divi?

No. We deliberately avoid bloated visual page builders that generate excessive DOM nodes, slow down mobile devices, and create security liabilities. Instead, we engineer custom WordPress themes using clean PHP/Gutenberg blocks, or build high-speed applications using React and Next.js.

WordPress vs React/Next.js — what is best for my company?

If you need regular content publishing, blog articles, and team self-management without coding, WordPress is an excellent choice. If you require interactive calculators, customer portals, custom dashboards, or sub-second headless performance, Next.js or React is superior. We advise on the best fit during our discovery call.

What materials do I need to prepare before we start?

Helpful items include your brand assets (logo, brand colors, typography guidelines), draft content/copy, high-resolution product or service photos, and any reference sites you admire. If you don't have copy prepared, we can guide your information architecture.

SEO, Hosting & Ownership

Will my website rank #1 on Google?

We never make dishonest promises of guaranteed #1 rankings, as Google's algorithm evaluates hundreds of evolving factors including domain age, competitor activity, and backlink authority. However, we guarantee best-practice technical SEO: perfect Core Web Vitals, semantic schema markup, clean URLs, mobile optimization, and proper meta data to give your business maximum organic potential.

Who owns the website and code after launch?

You do. You retain 100% legal ownership of your code, design assets, database, and content upon final payment. We can host it on your chosen cloud provider (AWS, Cloudflare, DigitalOcean, Vercel) or hand over a clean Git repository.

Do you provide ongoing website maintenance and security updates?

Yes. We offer optional monthly maintenance plans that cover regular security patching, database backups, uptime monitoring, and priority technical assistance.
