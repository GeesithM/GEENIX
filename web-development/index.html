Frequently Asked Questions

How much does web development cost in Sri Lanka?

Web development costs vary based on project complexity, features, design requirements, and technology stack. A standard business website typically ranges from LKR 50,000 to LKR 300,000+, while custom web applications and e-commerce platforms may cost more depending on scope. Contact us for a free consultation and personalised quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website or landing page typically takes 1 to 3 weeks. More complex web applications, custom platforms, or e-commerce stores generally take 3 to 6 weeks depending on features, third-party integrations, and revision cycles.

What technologies does GEENIX use?

We use production-ready technologies including React, Next.js, WordPress, WooCommerce, Node.js, and REST APIs. The technology stack is selected based on your project requirements, budget, performance needs, and long-term maintenance goals.

Do you offer website maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance, performance monitoring, security updates, and content updates. Post-launch support is available as needed or through a retainer arrangement.
