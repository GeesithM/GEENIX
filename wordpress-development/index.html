WordPress Development FAQ

Is WordPress suitable for business websites?

Yes. WordPress powers over 40% of all websites globally, from small business sites to enterprise platforms. With proper development, WordPress sites are fast, secure, SEO-friendly, and easy to maintain.

How much does a WordPress website cost in Sri Lanka?

Costs depend on design complexity, custom functionality, number of pages, and integrations. A standard business WordPress site typically starts from LKR 40,000. Contact us for a free consultation and detailed quote.

Can GEENIX build a WooCommerce store?

Yes. We build WooCommerce stores with secure checkout, payment gateway integration, optimised product pages, inventory management, and mobile-first shopping experiences.

WordPress vs custom development — which is better?

It depends on your needs. WordPress is ideal for content-driven sites, blogs, and standard business websites where you need easy content management. Custom development (React, Next.js) is better for complex web applications, unique interactivity, or high-performance requirements. We can help you choose the right approach.
