## Content Rules

- Every article must satisfy a **real search intent**
- Every article must provide **more practical value** than generic AI content
- No keyword stuffing, no filler paragraphs, no "In today's digital world..."
- Include **internal links** back to relevant service pages
- Include **real examples** and practical advice where possible
- Target **one primary keyword** per article (see [SEO_KEYWORD_MAP.md](SEO_KEYWORD_MAP.md))

## Internal Linking Strategy for Blog Posts

Every blog post should include:
- **1–2 links to its pillar service page** (e.g., WordPress article → `/wordpress-development/`)
- **1 link to a related blog article** within the same cluster
- **1 link to the contact page** as a contextual CTA
- Use **descriptive anchor text** (not "click here" or "learn more")
