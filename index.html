// 04 - Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, pricing, and process

What web development and design services does GEENIX provide?

GEENIX delivers end-to-end digital solutions including custom web development (React, Next.js, WordPress, Node.js), mobile-first responsive optimization and Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), UI/UX design with Figma prototyping, and technical SEO with Core Web Vitals performance optimization.

How much does a custom website development project cost?

Project pricing is customized based on your specific requirements, project scope, design complexity, and feature set. We offer competitive and transparent pricing for businesses and individuals. Contact us for a free, detailed project consultation and quote.

What is the typical timeline for building and launching a website?

A standard business website or landing page typically takes 1 to 3 weeks from discovery to deployment. More complex web applications, custom platforms, or e-commerce stores generally take 3 to 6 weeks depending on features and revision cycles.

Are all GEENIX websites optimized for mobile and SEO?

Yes. Every website built by GEENIX is engineered from first principles with responsive mobile-first architecture, fast load times for Google Core Web Vitals, structured schema data, semantic HTML5, and clean on-page SEO best practices.

How does the GEENIX development and design process work?

Our 4-step workflow begins with Discover (understanding your goals and audience), moves to Design (Figma wireframing and prototyping), proceeds to Build (clean, performance-first code), and finishes with Launch (deployment, SEO setup, and post-launch support).
